01 / THE PROBLEM

Why this project matters.

Learning platforms often separate explanations from practice and evidence of mastery. KHANIVERSITY combines authored coursework, interactive lab scenarios and an emerging account-backed learner journey.

Turn knowledge into sequenced, reviewable practice while keeping prototype evidence separate from official course records.

02 / THE EXECUTION

Design decisions that shape the build.

  1. Structure the learning journey

    Organize six schools and an 18-entry course catalog around a ten-module CS101 pathway and capstone.

  2. Make practice tangible

    Pair introductory Python CodeLab exercises with six simulated technology labs and formative assessment.

  3. Separate preview from credentials

    Distinguish browser-local evidence from authenticated Supabase grading, staff review and institutional course awards.

04 / CURRENT STATE

What is still being validated.

KHANIVERSITY is independent and does not award accredited degrees. Preview data is browser-local, labs are simulations, and account-backed services depend on correctly configured and security-tested Supabase infrastructure.
