01 / THE PROBLEM

Why this project matters.

Property management becomes difficult when lease status, maintenance requests, handover evidence and communication live in disconnected places. KhanHomes explores a coherent operations workspace that keeps those activities connected and auditable.

Bring property, tenancy, service and operational oversight into one navigable product.

02 / THE EXECUTION

Design decisions that shape the build.

  1. Model the real entities

    Shape the interface around organizations, properties, leases, residents and maintenance cases rather than decorative metrics.

  2. Protect sensitive workflows

    Use Supabase role boundaries and row-level security; treat authorization validation as a separate, mandatory release gate.

  3. Give operations context

    Introduce AURA Focus, maintenance work-order states and auditable move-in/out inspections while keeping unavailable data explicit.
